Design notes

Where animation earned its place: the orbiting dot and the floating stat card mirror the product itself — a session that rises and settles — so the motion explains the concept instead of just decorating it. Hover transitions on buttons and cards give quick, honest feedback that something is interactive, and they stay under 300ms so they never feel like they're stalling the reader. The features section reveals with the Intersection Observer only once, on first scroll into view, rather than re-triggering — repeating it would turn a moment of arrival into a distracting tic. Anything that looped constantly, shifted layout, or competed with the headline for attention got cut during review; the rule I kept was that if I could describe what an animation was proving, it stayed, and if I couldn't, it went.
